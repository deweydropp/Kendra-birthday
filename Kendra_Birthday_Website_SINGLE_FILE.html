The past 7 months with you have been some of the best of my life, and I genuinely don’t know where I’d be without you right now. I can’t imagine not being able to call you every day, hearing that sweet voice, your little giggles I always tell you I love, or talking you to sleep every night I can and hearing you slowly stop humming back to everything I say. Everything we do has slowly become something we do together, and I love it so much. I’ve always found myself getting sick of people after a while, hating staying on call, hating texting, but with you i’ve genuinely never loved it more, and im so thankful for all the memories we’ve shared together doing this. I tell you all the time about every single thing I love about you, but I genuinely cannot stop talking about it because you’re so so perfect Kendra. I love how hilarious you are, I love how you always manage to make me feel better after a rough day, I love how caring you are towards me and how much love you show me on the daily. I love how after every issue we’ve had you’ve tried to communicate with me and resolve it, even though its hard for the both of us. I love not only how much you’ve grown over the course of us knowing each other, but how much you’ve helped me grow. I swear we’re like slowly becoming one person. When i think about us, one of my favourite memories to look back on is from back when we were friends. I’ve told you this many times before, but I still think about this a lot. I remember secretly staying up reallyy late, and i couldn’t sleep. We just kept sending tiktoks back and forth to each other, and i swear on my life those were the funniest videos ever. I don’t know what it was, maybe it was how late it was getting, but i just keptt laughing, to the point where i started crying. I remember we both said that we were going to sleep several times, but we both couldn’t, and we just kept going back to each other. I distinctly remember thinking about how funny you were, like you were genuinely making me cryyy laughing dude. I think about that sometimes, and compare it to how we are now. We have nights like that all the time. And not just that, but more. We laugh together like that, we cry together, we talk about memories just like this, we talk about how much we love each other, we can be intimate together, and so so much more. It was so special to me that we were just laughing that hard with each other, and its so special to me now that this is such a normal thing. Anyway, I’m gonna cut this short because I do have a word limit, but i really hope this year brings you so much happiness, success, and everything you’ve been hoping for. Happy birthday again pretty girl, you’re genuinely my whole world and I never want you to forget that. You’re perfect, and i wouldn’t change a thing about you or our relationship. I want to love you like this forever, and I hope that when your next birthday rolls around, I can give you another letter just like this one. I love you sosoooo much and i can’t wait to see what this year brings you!!
